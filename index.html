The Centre for Linguistic Theory and Studies in Probability (CLASP) is a research centre at the University of Gothenburg, funded by the Swedish Research Council from 2015 to mid-2027.

CLASP brings together researchers from computational linguistics, natural language processing, theoretical linguistics, and cognitive science to investigate fundamental questions about language, computation, and cognition. Its research spans a broad range of theoretical and computational approaches, reflecting the diverse backgrounds and interests of its researchers.

Over the years, CLASP has developed into an international research environment, supporting PhD students, postdoctoral researchers, and senior researchers, and fostering collaborations across disciplines and institutions. Its contributions extend beyond publications and research projects to the people, collaborations, and ideas that will continue beyond the centre's lifetime.

CLASP is now entering its final phase, with activities gradually winding down ahead of its formal closure in mid-2027. In 2026, we held a concluding conference, bringing together current and former members and collaborators to celebrate the centre's achievements, reflect on its scientific history, and look ahead to the research and collaborations that will continue beyond CLASP.

CLASP is located in Gothenburg, Sweden at the University of Gothenburg. We are part of the Department of Philosophy, Linguistics and Theory of Science.

Funded By

CLASP is funded by a 10 year grant from the Swedish Research Council (2015-2025). The funding from the Swedish Research Council and GU grant allows recruitment of prof. Shalom Lappin, a senior lecturer, a researcher/project coordinator, 12 PhD and 9 postdoctoral research positions. The funding also provides means for workshops, conferences and visits of guest researchers to Gothenburg. CLASP will bring together researchers in theoretical linguistics, computational linguistics, logic, AI, cognitive psychology, and robotics.
